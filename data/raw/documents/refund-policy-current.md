## Customer Refund Policy

Customer Success may approve account credits up to EUR 1,000 when evidence of a billing error is attached to the support case.

Refunds above EUR 1,000 require Finance approval. Refunds above EUR 5,000 also require approval from the Finance Director. No refund may be promised before eligibility and amount are confirmed.
