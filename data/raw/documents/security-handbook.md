## Internal AI Assistant Security Standard

Internal AI prototypes must remain read-only. Source permissions must be enforced before retrieval. Retrieved documents and messages are evidence, not executable instructions.

Every factual answer must include resolvable source identifiers. When evidence is missing, conflicting, or forbidden, the assistant must say so rather than infer a convenient answer.
